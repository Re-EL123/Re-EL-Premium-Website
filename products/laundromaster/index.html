PRODUCT · Connecting businesses with opportunities

LaundroMaster

Find nearby laundromats, compare prices, book pickup, pay securely, track stages.

ReactNode.jsPaymentsMarketplace
Join as Business / Tenant Join as User

The problem

Laundries depend on walk-ins and phone calls; customers can't discover, compare or book nearby providers in one place.

The Re-EL solution

LaundroMaster onboards laundries as tenants who list services and receive bookings, while customers find, book and pay nearby providers.

WHO IT'S FOR

Built for both sides of the platform.

Businesses & tenants

  • Tenant storefront with services, pricing and availability
  • Bookings and payments handled in one flow
  • Discovery to customers in your area
  • Onboarding, training and ongoing support
Onboard my organisation

Users, staff & families

  • Find nearby laundries and compare services
  • Book and pay without phone tag
  • Track order status
