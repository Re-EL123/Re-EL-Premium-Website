COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

How much does a website cost in South Africa?

Re-EL projects start at R2,500 for a website, R5,000 for an application, R1,500 for automation and R2,500 per month for support. Every quote is scoped to your brief, so the final figure follows scope, complexity and requirements rather than a generic one-size-fits-all price.

What is the difference between the four tiers?

Websites covers corporate sites, landing pages and web apps engineered for performance and conversion. Applications covers custom platforms, business systems and internal tools. Automation covers repetitive workflow automation. Support covers ongoing maintenance, bug fixing and optimisation after launch.

Do you build custom software or use templates?

Custom. Platforms, business systems and internal tools are built around how your company actually works, and the interface is designed around real users so it feels fast, clear and trustworthy.

Can you connect the tools we already use?

Yes. API and integration work connects payments, CRMs, databases and third-party services to whatever we build, so your systems stop living in separate tabs.

What happens after the site or system goes live?

Technology does not stop at deployment. The support engagement covers ongoing maintenance, bug fixing and optimisation, starting at R2,500 per month.

How do we start a project?

Send the enquiry form below or message Re-EL on WhatsApp. The first step is a short scoping conversation so the problem is understood before anything is quoted.
